# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience.
Be considerate, communicate constructively, and focus feedback on ideas and work rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks are not acceptable. Project maintainers
may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe.

To report a concern, contact the project maintainers through the repository's private reporting
channel.
